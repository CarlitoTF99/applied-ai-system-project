# Road trip

Road trip music should carry you down the highway. Medium-to-high energy
(about 0.55 to 0.85) keeps the drive moving without becoming exhausting.
Country and folk songs about the open road, bright indie pop, synthwave for
night driving and classic rock singalongs are all staples, and carefree reggae
suits a slow coastal cruise. Valence is usually positive, so happy, hopeful,
carefree and nostalgic moods fit. Both acoustic and electric songs work.
