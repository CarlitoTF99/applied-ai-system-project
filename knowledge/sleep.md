# Sleep and winding down

Music for sleep should be as quiet and still as possible. Very low energy
(0.35 or below), slow tempos and no sudden changes help the body wind down.
Ambient soundscapes are ideal, followed by soft classical pieces, gentle lofi
and quiet jazz ballads. Valence should be calm and neutral rather than very
happy or very sad. Acoustic, instrumental, serene and dreamy songs with chill
or relaxed moods work best; avoid dramatic or intense tracks at bedtime.
