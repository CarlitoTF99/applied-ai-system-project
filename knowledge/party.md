# Party

Party music is about energy and good feelings shared with a crowd. Upbeat
pop, festive latin, confident hip-hop and bright indie pop keep people dancing,
and sunny reggae works for a laid-back daytime party. Target high energy (about
0.7 to 0.95) and high valence, since happy, euphoric songs beat dark ones at a
celebration. Electric, danceable production fits better than quiet acoustic
songs. Happy, energetic, carefree and confident moods are the best fit.
