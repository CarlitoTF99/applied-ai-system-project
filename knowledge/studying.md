# Studying and focus

Music for studying should stay in the background. Steady, low-energy tracks
with few or no vocals make it easier to concentrate on reading, coding or exam
prep. Lofi beats and ambient textures are the classic choice, and soft jazz or
calm classical pieces work for longer sessions. Aim for an energy of about 0.2
to 0.45 and a neutral-to-pleasant valence; sudden drops, loud choruses and
aggressive moods break focus. Acoustic, instrumental songs with focused, chill
or relaxed moods fit best.
