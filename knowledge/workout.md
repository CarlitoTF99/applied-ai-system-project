# Workout and gym

Workout music needs drive. High energy (0.8 and above) and a strong, steady
beat help with lifting, running and cardio. Intense pop anthems, confident
hip-hop, heavy rock and metal, and fast latin rhythms all keep the pace up.
Valence can vary: some people train to bright, euphoric tracks and others to
angry, aggressive ones. Production is almost always electric rather than
acoustic. Intense, energetic, confident and angry moods are the core of a gym
playlist.
