# Driven mood family (energetic, intense, confident, angry)

The driven family covers energetic, intense and confident songs, plus angry
ones from the catalog's turbulent family. They are loud and forceful, with
energy around 0.75 to 1.0 and almost always electric rather than acoustic
production. Rock and metal bring the aggression, hip-hop brings confidence,
and intense pop or energetic latin bring the adrenaline. Use this family for
hype, motivation and anything that should feel powerful.
