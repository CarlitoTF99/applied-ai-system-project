# Low mood family (sad, melancholy, moody, dramatic)

The low family covers sad, melancholy and moody songs, plus dramatic ones
from the catalog's turbulent family. Valence is low (about 0.1 to 0.4) and
energy is usually low to moderate. Slow blues is the classic sad genre,
hazy electronic and brooding synthwave carry melancholy and moodiness, and
wistful folk or stormy classical can be bittersweet or dramatic. Use this
family for heartbreak, loneliness or simply feeling down.
