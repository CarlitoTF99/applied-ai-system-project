# Calm mood family (chill, relaxed, focused)

The calm family covers the catalog moods chill, relaxed and focused. These
songs feel easy and unhurried: low-to-moderate energy (about 0.2 to 0.5), a
gently pleasant valence and usually acoustic or soft production. Lofi and
ambient are the most common calm genres, with jazz and laid-back reggae close
behind. "Calm" itself is a family name, not a song mood, so a calm request is
best matched to the mood chill.
