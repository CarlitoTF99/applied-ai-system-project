# Rainy day

A rainy day calls for cozy, unhurried music. Soft lofi loops, warm jazz,
slow blues and acoustic folk sit well with rain on the window and grey skies.
Energy usually stays low to moderate (about 0.25 to 0.5). Valence can lean
either way: a cozy chill or relaxed rainy afternoon, or a melancholy, sad,
lonely evening. Acoustic textures, gentle tempos and songs tagged rainy or
cozy match the mood best.
