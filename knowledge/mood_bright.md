# Bright mood family (happy, carefree, hopeful)

The bright family covers happy, carefree and hopeful songs. They have high
valence (0.7 and above) and moderate-to-high energy. Pop and indie pop are the
main bright genres, sunny reggae and festive latin add a carefree feel, and
country often carries a hopeful tone. Production can be acoustic or electric.
Use this family for feel-good, upbeat, summery or optimistic requests.
